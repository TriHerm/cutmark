## Recording

- Press Shift-Command-R to open Quick Recording. Choose camera, screen or both, select a microphone and set a countdown.
- Open a saved take directly in the timeline and resume the edit from the session later.

## Editing

- Camera and screen stay in separate source files. Linked clips use the recorded frame-time offset and follow screen trims, splits, moves and speed changes.
- Select a line in the transcript to jump to that moment or remove it. Review cuts suggested by Cutmark and choose which ones to apply as an undoable edit.
- Import a local animated GIF as a clip or overlay. The editor and MP4 export preserve the animation and composite transparent areas over the underlying video. Browse GIPHY opens in your browser.
