# Cutmark 0.1.1

Cutmark 0.1.1 focuses on recording reliability and keeping the recording workflow reachable outside the Cutmark window.

## Recording

- **Crash-recoverable camera and screen takes:** camera and screen movies are finalized in short fragments so completed media can survive an unexpected app or system interruption.
- **Interrupted-take recovery:** Cutmark keeps a durable per-take recording journal and automatically attaches playable camera or screen media the next time it opens.
- **Visible recovery confirmation:** when an interrupted take is restored, Cutmark tells you what was recovered.
- **Screen recording control strip:** Camera + Screen recordings now keep a compact Cutmark control strip above other apps and full-screen Spaces with the elapsed time, microphone level, selected display, and Stop & Save. The strip is excluded from the recorded screen.
- **Recording preflight:** screen-capture permission and critically low disk space are caught before the camera warm-up and countdown instead of failing after you are ready to record.
- **Stable screen timing:** synthetic static-screen filler and delayed ScreenCaptureKit frames can no longer regress video presentation timestamps.

## Editing foundation

- **Shared TimeMap:** source and output timing now use one mapping foundation for cuts and future non-destructive speed or event-driven edits.

## Reliability

- **Chat process completion:** Cutmark waits for the final process output to be delivered before a chat run is considered finished.
- **Current GitHub Actions runtimes:** release and CI actions use their current Node 24-compatible major versions.

## Platform

Cutmark 0.1.1 is a **macOS 15+ Apple silicon** release. The iPhone companion remains disabled in this release.

## Signing

The open-source release artifact is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized. macOS may require **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** on first launch.
