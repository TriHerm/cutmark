# From an idea to a saved video

1. Open a session or choose **Quick take**. In Record, **Recording setup** shows the selected camera, microphone, screen/window, and available space. A selected mic is not a sound test: turn on camera preview and speak to see a recent level. Screen-only capture can remain unchecked until you review a take.
2. Press Record. When the take has finished saving, the session offers **Edit take**. For a camera + screen recording, the first edit opens the screen file; the camera remains a separate saved source. You can open either source from its preview or the take menu.
3. In the editor, drag the ends of a section to trim. Move the red line and choose **Split**; select a section and choose **Remove**. **Undo** restores removed sections. **Redo** reapplies an undone change. **Changes** lists recent edits. Reordering and duplication are under **More**.
4. Choose **Create video**. Processing continues as a normal Cutmark job. The editor and session show its real status. Choose **Review video** when it finishes, play the result, then **Save version**. Saving uses Cutmark's existing checks and creates a numbered local version; failed checks remain blocked.
5. Choose **Export video…** to save a copy outside the session. Original recordings stay saved. An interrupted export leaves an existing destination intact.

On reopening a session, the status bar offers the next available action: continue a saved editing draft, review a finished render, or review/export a saved video. Failed or interrupted processing offers **Details**, with access to the existing Jobs controls and chat help.

The editing preview shows the cut. Effects, captions, framing, music, and processed voice may appear only in the created video; review that actual result before saving.

The visual reference for a focused preview and direct timeline was [Reelbird](https://github.com/btahir/reelbird). Cutmark continues to use its own native editor and render pipeline.
